          <h1 class="section__title">IBM Systems</h1>
        </div>

        <div class="readme-content">
          <p>
            My apprenticeship at AMETRAS dms managed service GmbH was built around IBM
            systems. Three years as an apprentice IBM Systems Engineer working with
            DB2, SAN storage, firewalls, backups, and switches.
          </p>
          <p>
            That's where I learned what enterprise reliability actually requires:
            backups you've tested restoring from, storage that's provisioned with
            headroom, and databases where a schema change gets planned, not
            improvised. DB2 administration taught me to think carefully before I touch
            data; SAN and storage work taught me that capacity planning is a real
            skill, not an afterthought.
          </p>
          <p>
            One of the things I'm proudest of from that apprenticeship: I built the
            monitoring setup myself. My supervisor scoped what needed watching —
            disk space, main processor load, VPN connections — and I wrote the
            scripts that gathered the data and formatted it for Nagios and Naemon to
            consume, then implemented the whole pipeline. More on that on the
            <a href="monitoring.html" class="link">Monitoring &amp; Alerting</a> page.
          </p>
          <p>
            It's a different world from the smaller, faster-moving projects I build
            outside of work, but the discipline carries over.
          </p>
        </div>
