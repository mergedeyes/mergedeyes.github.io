About Me

I work in IT systems engineering and I'm moving toward a DevOps role where I still get to write real code. Rust is my primary language; I reach for Python and Bash when the job calls for them.

I like understanding the why behind a design decision, not just the how; and I'd rather sit with a hard problem than paper over it. Lately that curiosity has been pulling me toward developing a socialmedia platform.
