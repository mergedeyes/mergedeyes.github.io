~/about

About Me

I work in IT systems engineering and I'm moving toward a DevOps role where I still get to write real code. Rust is my primary language; I reach for Go, Python, and C/C++ when the job calls for them.

I like understanding the why behind a design decision, not just the how — and I'd rather sit with a hard problem than paper over it. Lately that curiosity has been pulling me toward kernel development.

Outside of that, I run and maintain a few things of my own, and I'm always happy to talk about systems, infrastructure, and the trade-offs underneath.
